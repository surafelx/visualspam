Questions

Roofing questions we get every week

Can't see yours? Call 1300 000 000 and ask a roofer directly.

How much does a roof repair cost in Sydney?

Most leak repairs we do cost between $350 and $1,200, depending on access and what has failed. You get a fixed written price before any work starts.

Do you charge for roof inspections?

No. Inspections and quotes are free across our service area, and you get photos of everything we find.

Should I restore or replace my tile roof?

If the tiles and battens are sound, a restoration costs far less and adds 15 or more years. If battens are rotten or many tiles are porous, re-roofing in steel is usually better value. We'll show you photos and explain both options.

Can you come out for an emergency leak?

Yes. Call us and we'll make the roof watertight the same day where it's safe to go up, then quote the permanent fix.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold a NSW contractor licence for roofing and carry public liability and workers compensation insurance. Certificates are available on request.

What warranty do you give?

Every job carries our 10-year workmanship warranty, plus the manufacturer's warranty on materials.
