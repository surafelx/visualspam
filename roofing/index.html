Services

Four things, done well

We keep the list short on purpose. If your roof needs something else, ask and we'll tell you straight whether we're the right people.

Leaks & repairs

Cracked tiles, rusted sheets, failed flashings and valley irons. We find the source, not just the wet patch on the ceiling.

Most repairs done in one visit

Re-roofing

Tile or old iron replaced with new steel sheeting, sarking and insulation blanket. Pitches from 5° up.

Typical house: 3–5 days

Tile restoration

Pressure clean, re-bed and re-point ridge capping, replace broken tiles, then two coats of membrane.

Adds 15+ years to a sound tile roof

Gutters & downpipes

Quad, half-round and box gutters replaced or repaired, plus leaf guard fitted to suit bushland blocks.

Matched to your roof colour
